Descarte — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Descarte (“we,” “us,” “our”) collects and uses your information during our beta testing program.

1) Information We Collect

  • Your phone number (to send and receive SMS).
  • SMS content you send (used to generate grocery lists).
  • System logs and metadata (timestamps, basic usage stats).

2) How We Use Data

We use your data to provide and improve the grocery assistant service. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

3) Retention

Messages and logs may be retained temporarily for debugging and testing during beta. Data may be deleted after testing concludes.

4) Your Choices

You may opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP. Reply START to rejoin. For help, reply HELP.

5) Third-Party Services

We may integrate with grocers or payment providers. Their policies apply to their services.

6) Contact

For questions, contact: spencer@nocturnalsecurity.io

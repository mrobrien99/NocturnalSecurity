About Nocturnal Security

Founded by U.S. cyberwarfare veterans, Nocturnal Security is a modern defense and intelligence innovation lab building the tools that will power the next century of civilization.

We operate at the intersection of national security, decentralized infrastructure, and AI — creating dual-use technologies that operate in both contested and connected environments.

Our flagship products include:

  • Helionus – A modular, gravity-powered solar infrastructure designed for off-grid energy and surveillance.
  • Descartes – An SMS-first AI assistant for grocery ordering and logistics optimization.
  • Titan – A solar-powered aerial mesh node for ISR, crypto mining, and tactical comms in denied environments.
  • Bounce – A stealthy PowerShell toolset for agentless network discovery without ICMP.

We consult with select clients in cloud security architecture, AI system integration, SBIR strategy, and hardened infrastructure design.

Our Mission

To empower resilience at the edge — through autonomy, solar power, and secure software.

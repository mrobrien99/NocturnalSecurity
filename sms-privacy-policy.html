Descarte Messaging Program Privacy Policy

Descarte offers an SMS-based grocery concierge service. By opting in, you agree to this Policy.

What we collect

Your mobile phone number, message content you send us, and basic delivery/engagement metadata from our provider.

How we use it

To respond to your messages, operate the service, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information will not be sold, rented, or shared for marketing. We may share data with service providers (e.g., our messaging carrier/processor) solely to deliver the service.

Opt-in/Opt-out/Help

  • Opt-in by texting JOIN or any inquiry to +1 (888) 378-9655 (or our active number).
  • Reply STOP to cancel; START to rejoin; HELP for help.
  • Message frequency varies; Msg&Data rates may apply.

Security & retention

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures. We retain messages and metadata only as needed for the service and legal compliance.

Your choices & contact

You can opt out at any time via STOP. For privacy requests, contact: spencer@nocturnalsecurity.io.

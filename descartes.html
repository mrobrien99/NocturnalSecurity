Descartes

The SMS-based AI grocery ordering assistant that remembers your needs and shops for you — text to reorder, revise, and receive.

How It Works

Descartes learns your grocery patterns and sends you a smart shopping list via text each week.

  • 🛒 Get a weekly SMS with a pre-filled list based on your history
  • ✅ Reply “yes” to approve, or update items via text
  • 📦 Choose pickup or delivery via SMS
  • 💳 Checkout securely with Stripe

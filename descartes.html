Descartes

The SMS-based AI grocery assistant that learns your habits and sends what you need — before you realize you need it.

How It Works

Descartes sends you a smart shopping list via text each week, which you can modify, approve, and check out — all via SMS.

  • 🛒 Weekly AI-generated SMS shopping lists
  • ✅ Reply “yes” or edit items via text
  • 📦 Choose pickup or delivery
  • 💳 Pay securely via Stripe
